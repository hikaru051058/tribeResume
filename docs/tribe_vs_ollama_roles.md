# TRIBE v2 vs Ollama Roles

| Layer | What It Does | What It Does Not Do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| TRIBE v2 | Predicts stimulus-response-like patterns from text/audio/video/document stimuli. Useful for salience, attention, and cognitive-load hypotheses. | Does not perform semantic hiring judgment. Does not prove real recruiter perception. Does not directly say a resume is good or bad. |
| Ollama | Interprets language, explains proxy signals, simulates reviewer personas, and suggests edits. | Can hallucinate, be generic, overrate claims, or miss weak evidence. It is not the core perception signal by itself. |
| Combined | TRIBE gives an experimental perception signal; Ollama turns it into cautious explanation and action. | Still does not predict real hiring outcomes or actual brain activity. |

## Correct Framing

Use cautious language:

- May suggest.
- Proxy signal.
- Perception hypothesis.
- Predicted response pattern.
- Experimental signal.

Avoid:

- Predicts hiring outcomes.
- Measures actual perception.
- Proves recruiter reaction.
- TRIBE says the resume is good or bad.
